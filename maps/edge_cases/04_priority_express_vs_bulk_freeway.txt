# Tests Dijkstra cost weighing and high-concurrency pipe balancing.
# Route A consists of priority zones (cost=5) but has single drone capacity (max_drones=1, max_link_capacity=1).
# Route B consists of normal zones (cost=10) but has heavy throughput capacity (max_drones=3, max_link_capacity=3).
# The pathfinder should allocate drones between the fast narrow express lane and the wide bulk freeway,
# verifying that connection concurrency of 3 drones per turn operates safely alongside 1-drone lanes.
